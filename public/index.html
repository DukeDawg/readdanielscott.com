About Daniel Scott

Thrillers where the case is only the visible wound.

Daniel Scott writes for readers who want stories that move fast, hit hard, and leave a bruise. The books move from FBI serial-killer investigations to psychological descent, escaped-monster manhunts, and haunted-family suspense.

The connective tissue is simple: dangerous people, damaged heroes, old sins, and the suspicion that solving the case may not be the same thing as surviving it.
